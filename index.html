From floor plan to show day.

Step 1 of 6

  1. Brief and site check

    We read the hall plan, organiser rules, rigging points and power before drawing anything, so the design is buildable on the first try.

  2. Design and 3D

    Concepts and photoreal 3D views with real dimensions. You see the stand from the aisle, exactly as visitors will.

  3. Fabrication

    Walls, counters, towers and graphics are built and finished in our workshop, then test-assembled before they ship.

  4. Installation and rigging

    Our crew builds on site, hangs truss and lighting and signs off every snag with your team before handover.

  5. Show days

    A floor manager stays on call through the show for touch-ups, AV and anything that needs fixing fast.

  6. Dismantle and storage

    We break down on schedule, clear the hall and store reusable elements for your next show.
